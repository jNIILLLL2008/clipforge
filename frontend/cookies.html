The short version

  • One cookie keeps you signed in. Without it there is no way to stay logged in, so it is not optional and we do not pretend to ask.
  • Anything that is not strictly necessary is off until you say yes, and refusing costs you nothing.
  • We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell or share what we measure.

You can change your answer at any time with the cookie preferences link, kept in the footer of every page.

Strictly necessary

These are needed for the service to work at all. Under the ePrivacy rules and the UK's PECR, storage that is strictly necessary to provide a service the user has asked for does not require consent — so there is no toggle for these. What there is instead is this list.

NameTypeWhat it doesExpires
cf_token First-party cookie Your signed-in session. Set when you log in, cleared when you log out. Marked HttpOnly so no script can read it, SameSite=Lax so another site cannot use it, and Secure in production. 14 days
cf_consent Local storage Remembers the answer you gave below, so you are not asked on every page. It is stored locally and never sent to the server. 12 months

Blocking the first one means you cannot sign in. Blocking the second means the notice reappears each visit, which is the safe way for it to fail.

What we do not use

  • No advertising or retargeting pixels. No Meta pixel, no TikTok pixel, no Google Ads tag.
  • No cross-site tracking, and no data broker or "audience" integrations.
  • No session recording or heat-mapping tools that replay what you did on the page.
  • No fingerprinting to identify a browser that has refused storage.

Changing your mind

Use the cookie preferences link in the footer. Withdrawing consent is the same number of clicks as giving it, and takes effect immediately.

Your browser can also block or clear cookies for this site directly, in its privacy settings. Clearing site data removes both entries in the table above, which signs you out and brings the notice back.
